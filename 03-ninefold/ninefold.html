    Ninefold

    A puzzle in five acts.

    • Click or drag to select cells. Ctrl/Shift-click adds to the selection; double-click selects matching digits.
    • Type digits. Hold Shift for corner marks and Ctrl for centre marks, or switch modes with Z X C V.
    • Backspace clears. Ctrl+Z undoes, Ctrl+Y redoes. Arrow keys move.
    • Tap a cell, or drag across several, then tap a digit on the keypad. Double-tap a filled cell to select every cell with the same digit.
    • For pencil marks and colours, switch the keypad with the Corner, Centre and Colour buttons; Digit switches back. Delete clears the digit first, then the marks.
    • Each act grows in stages: once you have placed the digits the current rules let you work out, new rules appear. Stuck? Turn on Seed hints here: sprouts then mark those cells, and the rules header counts how many you have filled.
    • Hover over a rule to light up the clues it applies to.
